HELPFUL INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions.

A few quick answers before your next journey.

Is there a return fare for one-way trips?

No. One-way trips are charged at ₹15 per kilometre without return fare.

What is the round-trip rate?

Round-trip travel is ₹14 per kilometre. Driver bata, toll and permit charges may apply.

Do you provide airport transfers?

Yes. Airport pickup and drop service is available for Chennai and Trichy airports from Thiruppanandal.

How can I book?

Call us or send a WhatsApp message with your trip details. Availability and final fare will be confirmed.

Is share taxi available?

Yes. Share taxi options are available for Chennai and Trichy airport routes. Contact us for details.

Are toll and permit charges included?

Toll and permit charges are additional where applicable. The final fare will be confirmed before your trip.
