HELPFUL INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions.

A few quick answers before your next journey.

Is there a return fare for one-way trips?

No. One-way trips are charged per kilometre without return fare (Small Car ₹15, SUV ₹20, Innova ₹22). Minimum 130 km applies for one-way trips.

What is the round-trip rate?

Round-trip rate is ₹14 per kilometre for Small Car, ₹20 for SUV and ₹22 for Innova, with a minimum of 250 km per day. Driver bata, toll and permit charges may apply.

Do you provide airport transfers?

Yes. Airport pickup and drop service is available for Chennai and Trichy airports with Kumbakonam pickup.

How can I book?

Call us or send a WhatsApp message with your trip details. Mabrook Travels is an online booking service, and your ride is arranged with a Kumbakonam driver. Availability and final fare will be confirmed.

Is share taxi available?

Yes. Share taxi options are available for Chennai and Trichy airport routes. Contact us for details.

Are toll and permit charges included?

Toll and permit charges are additional where applicable. The final fare will be confirmed before your trip.
